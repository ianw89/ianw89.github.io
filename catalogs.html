DESI BGS Data Release 1 Galaxy Group Catalog

This is a catalog of 2,229,741 galaxies from the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) Bright Galaxy Survey (BGS) Data Release 1 (DR1). The galaxies are location in ~2680 deg2 of sky with 3-pass coverage and r-band magnitudes <19.5. The catalog provides dark matter halo mass estimates and central/satellite classifications for galaxies in the flux-limited sample. This catalog is DESI DR1 Value Added Catalog.
